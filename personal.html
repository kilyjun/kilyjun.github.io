I spend most of my listening time on classical, like Elgar's cello concerto, Saint-Saëns, and Chopin's études. I liked rock more in middle and high school, when I would try to play songs like Bon Jovi, Coldplay and Radiohead. These days I am increasingly listening to and discovering instrumentals and soundtracks.

Across genres, I keep being drawn to something unhurried, usually with a piano at its centre, that opens with grandness and builds until it feels much larger than where it began.
