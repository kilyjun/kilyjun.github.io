Junhyeok Kil

I'm a 1st year PhD student in Quantitative Methods at Purdue University, advised by Tongseok Lim.

I like to think about problems involving higher-order interactions in networks and randomness on graphs, with the goal of applications in business, engineering, and AI.

My undergrad was also at Purdue, where I majored in Data Science, Mathematics, and Statistics and worked at the Composites Manufacturing & Simulation Center with Eduardo Barocio and Akshay Jacob Thomas, learning about physics-informed machine learning and simulation optimization for additive manufacturing.

I was born in South Korea, grew up in Singapore, and now live in Indiana.

Reading was most active in 2021–2022 when I was in the Korean army. It was an amazing time to open my eyes to maths and philosophy.

I spend most of my listening time on classical, like Elgar's cello concerto, Saint-Saëns, and Chopin's études. I liked rock more in middle and high school, when I would try to play songs by Bon Jovi and Radiohead. These days I am increasingly listening to and discovering instrumentals and soundtracks. My all-time favourite soundtrack is The Cloud Atlas Sextet.

Across genres, I keep being drawn to something unhurried, usually with a piano at its centre, that opens with grandness and builds until it feels much larger than where it began.

I used to make some myself when I was more passionate. A few tracks are still on SoundCloud.

This is a collection of pieces that I love, that I share with people around me, and that shaped me.
